## AlgoZenith DSA Library

A lightweight DSA Library preview platform that enables the internal team to browse, upload, and review Markdown/MDX-based learning content in a clean, structured interface. The app organizes content into Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced DSA tracks, with topic-wise pages displaying explanations, embedded videos, quizzes, code examples, and practice problems exactly as learners would see them.
